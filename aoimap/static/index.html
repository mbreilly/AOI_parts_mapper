MODELS

Three small models, one for each job of the check, trained on this machine from your answers (GOOD, BAD, MISSING, NS) and from the reference board. They give an extra opinion with a confidence (on Check a board and Look at a part, as "Models think"); the check's own answer is still what counts. Retrain after teaching more parts.

Accuracy is tested on parts each model wasn't trained on: the examples of each part are kept out in turn (5 rounds) and it's the share it got right. "Guessing" is what always giving the most common answer would get. Auto-made examples (the reference board's parts, turned copies of right ones, bare DNP pads) fill in until there are enough of your own answers. Accuracy measured mostly on auto-made examples is a rough guide until more real answers exist. A model's opinion is shown only once it has enough examples and 75% accuracy or more.
